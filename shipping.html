Handcrafting Production Schedule

Because our nail sets are handcrafted works of art rather than mass injection-molded plastics, please note the following production lead times:

  • Ready-to-Ship Sets & Prep Kits: Dispatched from our atelier within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Made-to-Order Standard Sizes (XS, S, M, L): Require 2 to 3 days for 5-layer gel sculpting and UV diamond curing.
  • Custom Millimeter Sizing & 1-of-1 Commissions: Require 3 to 5 days for bespoke tailoring and artist review.
