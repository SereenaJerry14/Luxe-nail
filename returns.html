Sanitary & Hygiene Return Standards

Due to strict personal cosmetic hygiene standards and health regulations, nail sets that have been opened, unsealed, or applied to natural fingernails cannot be returned for a cash refund.

If your order arrives in brand-new, unopened, and factory-sealed condition with the velvet presentation case intact, you may initiate a return within 14 days of delivery. Once the returned parcel arrives at our atelier and passes physical Quality Check (QC), a 100% refund is reversed to your original payment method or UPI ID.

How to Initiate a Return or Size Adjustment

  1. Use our Self-Service Refund Portal above or email our concierge at concierge@luxeclaws.com.
  2. Our courier will collect the package from your address with a prepaid return tracking label.
  3. Upon receipt at our atelier, our master artisan conducts a Quality Check (QC) inspection.
  4. Once QC approval is complete, your refund is transferred directly to your UPI ID or Card within 24 hours.
