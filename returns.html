Returns & Perfect Fit Guarantee

We stand behind the flawless craftsmanship and tailored fit of every handcrafted set.

Our 30-Day Perfect Fit Guarantee

We want you to feel utterly radiant in your LUXECLAWS. If you ordered standard sizing (XS, S, M, L) and find that 1 or 2 nails do not fit your natural nail beds comfortably, our atelier will sculpt and dispatch complimentary replacement sizing nails to your doorstep free of charge!

Sanitary & Hygiene Return Standards

Due to strict personal cosmetic hygiene standards and health regulations, nail sets that have been opened, unsealed, or applied to natural fingernails cannot be returned for a refund.

If your order arrives in brand-new, unopened, and factory-sealed condition with the velvet presentation case intact, you may initiate a return within 14 days of delivery for a full refund or atelier store credit.

Single-Nail Replacement Service

Accidentally misplaced an accent nail during travel? There is no need to re-purchase the entire set! Registered VIP members can request single replacement nails sculpted to match their exact design archive for only $7.00 per nail.

How to Initiate a Return or Size Adjustment

  1. Email our concierge at concierge@luxeclaws.com with your order number (e.g. ORD-894210).
  2. Include a clear photograph of your natural nail bed measurements or unopened packaging.
  3. Our concierge will issue a prepaid return shipping label or confirm immediate replacement dispatch.
