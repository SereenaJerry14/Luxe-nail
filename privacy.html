Privacy & Data Security Policy

Your trust and digital confidentiality are fundamental to our atelier standard of service.

1. Information We Collect

To craft and deliver your personalized nail sets, we collect the following client data:

  • Account & Contact Info: Full name, email address, shipping street address, and phone number for courier delivery notifications.
  • Nail Sizing Profiles: Millimeter nail bed widths, shape preferences, and custom calibration notes saved to your profile.
  • Encrypted Payment Data: Processed directly via PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways. We never store raw credit card numbers on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to fulfill orders, provide customer concierge support, deliver package tracking notifications, and tailor recommendations for your saved nail sizing. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers under any circumstances.

3. GDPR & CCPA Client Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to request access to, download, or permanently delete your personal account data at any time by contacting privacy@luxeclaws.com.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We utilize local storage and essential session cookies to remember your cart items, wishlist selections, dark/light theme preference, and authenticated VIP login state.
