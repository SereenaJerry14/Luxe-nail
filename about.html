The Philosophy of LUXECLAWS

Founded at the intersection of Tokyo nail sculpting discipline and Beverly Hills red-carpet aesthetics, LUXECLAWS was born from a singular conviction: you should never have to sacrifice natural nail health or waste 3 hours sitting at a salon table for show-stopping nail artistry.

Unlike mass-produced plastic press-ons found in drugstores, every single LUXECLAWS set is individually painted by master nail artists using multiple layers of professional salon-grade Japanese builder gel, high-density metallic chromes, and precision-placed genuine Swarovski crystals.

Our 7-Layer Sculpting Process

01. Soft-Gel Base

Molded from flexible soft gel that contours seamlessly against natural nail c-curves without cracking or pinching.

02. Apex Reinforcement

Double-cured hard builder gel along the stress apex to prevent bending under pressure during everyday tasks.

03. Handcrafted Artistry

Micro-airbrush shading, metallic cat-eye magnetic alignment, and hand-sculpted 3D relief emblems.

04. Diamond Top Coat

Stain-resistant, high-gloss non-wipe seal that maintains mirror reflection for 3+ weeks without dulling.
