2. Handcrafted Artistry & Natural Variations

Each set of LUXECLAWS press-on nails is individually hand-sculpted, painted, and cured by our master nail artisans. Clients are responsible for providing accurate millimeter nail bed measurements or selecting the appropriate standard size (XS, S, M, L) as guided by our Sizing Chart. While our 30-Day Perfect Fit Guarantee provides free replacement sizing nails for standard sizes, custom 1-of-1 commission sets are sculpted precisely to the millimeter measurements submitted by the client upon checkout.
