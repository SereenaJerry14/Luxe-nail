Nail Sizing Made Effortless

Ensure a seamless, salon-flush fit with our 2-minute sizing guide and instant measurement calculator.

1

Place Tape Over Widest Part

Place transparent tape horizontally across the widest point of your bare nail bed.

2

Mark the Sidewalls

Use a fine pen to mark both outer edges where the nail meets the skin.

3

Measure with Ruler in Millimeters (mm)

Remove the tape, place against a metric ruler, and note each finger's mm width.
